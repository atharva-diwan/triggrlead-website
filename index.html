Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need to change our existing tools?

No. The system wraps around what you already use — WhatsApp, your CRM, Google Drive, Razorpay, Practive. Your team doesn't change anything.

We already have staff handling these tasks.

Most firms do. The issue is that those people spend 60% of their time on tasks that don't require a human. The system handles the repetitive work so your team can focus on what actually needs them.

How is our client data protected?

All AI processing uses private API endpoints — no client data passes through consumer tools like ChatGPT. Data stays within your Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 tenant. Every agent action is logged with full audit trail.
